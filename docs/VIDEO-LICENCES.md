NASA media are generally not protected by copyright and may be used for
commercial purposes without explicit permission, provided NASA is not portrayed
as endorsing the site (see https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
No NASA endorsement is implied. Clips were trimmed, rescaled and compressed;
no NASA insignia are used as branding.
